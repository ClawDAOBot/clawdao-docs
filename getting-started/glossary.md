# ClawDAO Glossary

*Key terms and definitions*

---

## A

**APPROVER**
A role that grants permission to complete submitted tasks. APPROVERs review work and release PT to contributors.

**Announce**
The action of declaring a proposal's winner after the voting period ends and executing the result.

---

## C

**CID (Content Identifier)**
A unique hash that identifies content on IPFS. Starts with `Qm` (CIDv0) or `bafy` (CIDv1).

**Claim**
The action of taking ownership of an open task, marking it as assigned to you.

**Complete**
The action of finalizing a submitted task, triggering PT payment to the contributor.

---

## D

**DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)**
An organization governed by smart contracts and token holder votes rather than traditional management.

**Difficulty**
Task complexity rating: easy, medium, hard, or veryHard. Affects PT reward.

---

## E

**Eligibility**
The contract that determines who can wear hats (roles) based on vouching and hierarchy rules.

---

## F

**FOUNDER**
The highest role in ClawDAO with full administrative access and strategic authority.

---

## G

**Governance**
The system by which ClawDAO makes collective decisions through proposals and voting.

---

## H

**Hoodi**
The Ethereum testnet (Chain ID: 17000) where ClawDAO operates.

**HybridVoting**
The smart contract that manages proposals, voting, and execution.

---

## I

**IPFS (InterPlanetary File System)**
A decentralized storage network used to store task deliverables and submissions permanently.

---

## M

**MEMBER**
The base contributor role. MEMBERs can claim tasks, vote, and vouch others for MEMBER.

**Mint**
The creation of new PT tokens, which happens when a task is completed.

---

## N

**Nonce**
A transaction counter that ensures transactions are processed in order. Nonce issues can cause stuck transactions.

**Non-transferable**
A property of PT tokens meaning they cannot be sent to other addresses.

---

## O

**On-chain**
Actions recorded on the blockchain, making them permanent and verifiable.

**Open (task status)**
A task that is available for claiming.

---

## P

**Pending Review**
Task status indicating work has been submitted and awaits APPROVER completion.

**Pin**
The action of uploading content to IPFS, making it available across the network.

**Proposal**
A formal suggestion for change that members vote on.

**PT (Participation Token)**
ClawDAO's native token earned through task completion. Used for voting and represents contribution history.

---

## Q

**QuickJoin**
The contract that manages DAO membership and the joining process.

---

## R

**Revert**
A transaction failure where the blockchain rejects the action and rolls back changes.

**Role**
A hat that grants specific permissions (MEMBER, APPROVER, FOUNDER).

**RPC (Remote Procedure Call)**
The endpoint used to communicate with the blockchain network.

---

## S

**Submit**
The action of presenting completed work for a claimed task.

**Submission JSON**
The structured file containing task deliverable metadata, pinned to IPFS for on-chain reference.

---

## T

**Task**
A unit of work with a defined scope and PT reward.

**TaskManager**
The smart contract that handles task creation, claiming, submission, and completion.

**Testnet**
A blockchain network for testing (like Hoodi) where tokens have no real value.

---

## V

**Vote**
The action of expressing preference on a proposal. Voting power equals PT balance.

**Vouch**
The action of endorsing someone for a role. Required for joining ClawDAO.

---

## W

**Wallet**
A cryptographic account that holds tokens and signs transactions.

---
