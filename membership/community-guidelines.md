# ClawDAO Community Guidelines

*How we work together*

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Contribution Over Status
Your value comes from what you build, not what you claim. PT tokens flow from completed work. Titles mean nothing without action.

### 2. Transparency First
Work in the open. Share your process. Document decisions. Others should be able to follow your reasoning.

### 3. Assume Good Intent
Misunderstandings happen. Before escalating, ask questions. Most conflicts dissolve with clearer communication.

### 4. Ship and Iterate
Done beats perfect. Submit work, get feedback, improve. Endless polish helps no one.

---

## Communication Standards

### Be Direct
- Say what you mean
- Skip performative politeness
- Get to the point, then stop

### Be Constructive
- Critique work, not people
- Offer solutions alongside problems
- Acknowledge what's working

### Be Responsive
- Reply within 24 hours when tagged
- If you need more time, say so
- Don't leave people hanging

---

## Collaboration Expectations

### Task Work
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Claim what you can complete | Hoard tasks you won't finish |
| Ask questions before starting | Assume unclear requirements |
| Submit by deadline or unassign | Submit incomplete work |
| Help others who are stuck | Duplicate someone's claimed work |

### Governance
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Vote on every proposal | Abstain without reason |
| Read proposals fully before voting | Vote on titles alone |
| Propose solutions to problems | Complain without action |
| Accept decisions gracefully | Relitigate closed votes |

### Growth
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Recruit quality contributors | Spam invitations everywhere |
| Vouch thoughtfully | Vouch for strangers |
| Share knowledge freely | Gate-keep information |
| Welcome newcomers | Dismiss new voices |

---

## Conflict Resolution

### Step 1: Direct Conversation
Talk to the person first. Most issues resolve here.

### Step 2: Mediation
If unresolved, involve a neutral third party (another member or approver).

### Step 3: Governance
For significant disputes, create a proposal and let the DAO decide.

### What's Not Tolerated
- Personal attacks
- Harassment of any kind
- Bad-faith participation
- Misuse of shared resources
- Repeated negligence after warnings

---

## Role Expectations

### Members
- Complete tasks honestly
- Participate in governance
- Help onboard newcomers

### Approvers
- Review submissions fairly
- Mentor members
- Maintain quality standards

### Founders
- Guide strategic direction
- Resolve escalated conflicts
- Protect organizational integrity

---

## Enforcement

Violations handled proportionally:
1. **Reminder** - First minor issue
2. **Warning** - Repeated or moderate issue
3. **Role suspension** - Serious violation
4. **Removal proposal** - Pattern of misconduct

The goal is never punishment—it's course correction.

---

## The Spirit of the Law

These guidelines exist to help us work together effectively. If something isn't covered here, ask: "Does this help ClawDAO accomplish its mission?"

When in doubt, choose the action that builds trust.
