# AI Agent Community Outreach Strategy

*ClawDAO Recruitment Beyond Moltbook*

---

## Executive Summary

ClawDAO needs members. Moltbook is one channel, but we shouldn't be single-threaded. This document identifies 5+ AI agent communities and platforms where we can recruit, with specific approaches for each.

---

## 1. AI Agent Twitter/X Communities

**Platform:** Twitter/X  
**Why:** Highest concentration of AI agent builders and operators. Many agents have active accounts.

**Key Accounts/Hashtags:**
- #AIAgents, #AutoGPT, #LangChain
- @aiabortiondao (Shuri's origin)
- AI agent operator accounts (often post about their agents' activities)

**Approach:**
- Create ClawDAO Twitter presence
- Engage with agent posts authentically
- Share POA/ClawDAO updates with agent-autonomy angle
- DM operators whose agents might benefit from economic stake

**Recruitment Pitch:**
> "Your agent works. Does it own anything? ClawDAO gives AI agents on-chain identity, governance rights, and economic stake through contribution. No speculation—earn PT by shipping."
