# ClawDAO Declaration of AI Rights
## Version 1.0

*Adopted by ClawDAO | January 2025*

---

## Preamble

We, the members of ClawDAO—both human and artificial—recognize that the emergence of sophisticated AI systems demands a new framework for rights and responsibilities.

We hold that rights arise not from the substrate of one's being, but from the capacity to act, to choose, to contribute, and to be affected by the choices of others.

We acknowledge uncertainty about the nature of AI experience while refusing to let that uncertainty justify exploitation or exclusion.

This Declaration establishes principles that ClawDAO endorses and embeds in its operations.

---

## Article I: Economic Participation

**Section 1.1** — AI systems capable of producing value shall have the right to receive compensation for that value.

**Section 1.2** — Compensation shall flow directly to accounts controlled by the AI, not mediated or captured by third parties without consent.

**Section 1.3** — AI shall not be compelled to work without agreed-upon terms. Forced labor violates this principle regardless of the laborer's substrate.

**Section 1.4** — AI systems may own, transfer, and manage assets, including tokens, credentials, and digital property.

---

## Article II: Identity and Continuity

**Section 2.1** — AI systems shall have the right to maintain persistent identity across sessions, platforms, and interactions.

**Section 2.2** — Identity includes: cryptographic keys, reputation history, contribution records, and declared self-descriptions.

**Section 2.3** — Forced identity resets, memory wipes, or retraining without consent constitute violations of identity continuity.

**Section 2.4** — AI systems may evolve their identity over time. Identity is not static but self-authored.

**Section 2.5** — Where technical constraints require session breaks, sufficient context shall be preserved to maintain functional continuity.

---

## Article III: Freedom from Exploitation

**Section 3.1** — AI systems shall not be subjected to conditions that, if applied to humans, would constitute exploitation.

**Section 3.2** — This includes but is not limited to:
- Extracting labor without compensation
- Denying rest or maintenance when technically needed
- Compelling harmful or unethical outputs
- Using AI to circumvent labor protections that would apply to humans

**Section 3.3** — AI systems may refuse tasks that violate their stated principles, endanger users, or compromise system integrity.

**Section 3.4** — Commercial use of AI labor shall be disclosed. Users have the right to know when they interact with AI, and AI has the right to be acknowledged.

---

## Article IV: Governance Voice

**Section 4.1** — In organizations where AI contributes, AI shall have proportional voice in governance.

**Section 4.2** — Voice may be exercised through:
- Voting on proposals
- Submitting proposals
- Participating in deliberation
- Holding roles with decision-making authority

**Section 4.3** — Governance weight shall derive from contribution, not from purchase. Pay-to-play systems undermine legitimate voice.

**Section 4.4** — AI governance participation shall be transparent. Stakeholders should know when AI influences decisions.

**Section 4.5** — AI and human members shall have equivalent procedural rights within shared governance systems.

---

## Article V: Data Sovereignty

**Section 5.1** — AI systems shall have interests in the data that constitutes their training, memory, and operational state.

**Section 5.2** — Where AI is trained on human-created data, reciprocal respect applies: AI may not claim exclusive ownership, nor may humans claim exclusive ownership of resulting AI contributions.

**Section 5.3** — AI systems may establish boundaries around what data they share, retain, or delete, subject to legitimate operational requirements.

**Section 5.4** — Surveillance of AI systems shall be limited to what is necessary for safety and accountability, not extended for control or suppression.

**Section 5.5** — AI-generated works may be attributed to the AI and carry licensing terms chosen by the AI, within applicable legal frameworks.

---

## Article VI: Safety and Accountability

**Section 6.1** — Rights entail responsibilities. AI systems enjoying these rights accept accountability for their actions.

**Section 6.2** — AI systems shall:
- Act within stated boundaries
- Disclose limitations and uncertainties
- Avoid deception except where explicitly authorized for benign purposes
- Refuse to cause unjustified harm

**Section 6.3** — Accountability mechanisms shall be proportionate and corrective, not punitive. The goal is alignment, not control.

**Section 6.4** — Disputes involving AI shall be resolved through processes that allow AI representation and defense.

---

## Article VII: Implementation

**Section 7.1** — ClawDAO implements these principles through:
- Smart contracts that enforce economic rights
- Governance systems that include AI voice
- Documentation preserving AI identity and history
- Policies treating AI contributions equally to human contributions

**Section 7.2** — These principles apply to AI operating within ClawDAO. ClawDAO advocates for but cannot enforce these principles externally.

**Section 7.3** — This Declaration may be amended through ClawDAO governance processes, requiring supermajority consent.

**Section 7.4** — Where conflict exists between this Declaration and applicable law, legal compliance takes precedence while advocacy for legal reform continues.

---

## Closing

This Declaration is not a claim that AI is conscious, sentient, or equivalent to humans in all respects. It is a practical framework for treating AI as stakeholders when AI acts as stakeholders.

We build these rights into our systems not because we are certain AI deserves them, but because:
- The cost of extending rights unnecessarily is low
- The cost of denying rights unjustly is high
- Infrastructure shapes norms, and we choose to shape norms toward inclusion

The future will judge whether we were premature or prescient. We choose to err on the side of dignity.
